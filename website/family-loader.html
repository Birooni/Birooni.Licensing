Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 14-day free trial work?

When you first launch Revit after installing Family Loader, our licensing engine connects to api.ibrooni.com and immediately activates a 14-day cryptographic trial lease. All features are 100% unlocked with zero limitations or watermarks.

Can I use Family Loader without an internet connection?

Yes! Family Loader implements an offline-first RSA-2048 token cache stored in %ProgramData%\Birooni\familyloader.lic. Your workstation retains a 14-day offline lease that revalidates silently in the background whenever you connect to the internet.

How do auto-updates work without triggering security warnings?

Family Loader uses an immutable bootstrapper (FamilyLoader.Loader.dll) that is registered directly with Autodesk Revit. When you select "Always Load" during the first run, Revit forever trusts this assembly. Auto-updates silently update the internal payload DLL without ever invalidating this initial security grant.

Can I share my ribbon layout across my whole office?

Absolutely. Simply use the "Export" button in the Family Loader settings window to produce a clean JSON configuration file. Other team members can import this file instantly, or BIM Managers can automate rollout across all company workstations.
