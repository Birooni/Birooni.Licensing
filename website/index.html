Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 14-day free trial work?

When you first install BiruBox and launch Autodesk Revit, our licensing engine automatically detects your device and generates a 14-day cryptographic trial lease via api.ibrooni.com. All features are 100% unlocked with no watermarks or limitations.

Can I use BiruBox without an internet connection?

Yes! BiruBox uses an offline-first RSA-2048 token cache. Once activated, your workstation retains a 14-day offline lease that revalidates silently in the background whenever an internet connection is detected. If you are traveling or on a restricted site, your tools continue running without interruption.

Can I transfer my perpetual license to a new PC?

Yes. You can deactivate your license directly within Revit using the BiruBox License Settings dialog, or through the central customer portal. Once deactivated, the seat is immediately freed and ready for activation on your new machine.

How are updates delivered?

BiruBox includes an automated update checker. When a new version or hotfix is published to our release registry, a subtle notification badge appears in Revit prompting you to update with one click.
